## Park Features

Riverside Nature Park spans over 200 acres of pristine natural habitat along the scenic river. Our facilities include:

- **Trail System**: 8 miles of well-maintained trails ranging from easy to moderate difficulty
- **Picnic Areas**: Multiple covered pavilions available for reservation
- **Wildlife Viewing**: Observation decks perfect for birdwatching and photography
- **Playground**: Modern play equipment for children of all ages

## Activities

The park is ideal for hiking, jogging, cycling, picnicking, and nature photography. Fishing is permitted in designated areas with a valid state license.

## Visitor Information

Free parking is available at three locations throughout the park. Restroom facilities are located near each parking area. Dogs are welcome but must be kept on leash at all times.

## Seasonal Highlights

- **Spring**: Wildflower blooms and migrating birds
- **Summer**: Lush greenery and river activities
- **Fall**: Spectacular foliage displays
- **Winter**: Peaceful snow-covered landscapes and cross-country skiing
