## What to Expect

The Historic Town Museum features three floors of carefully curated exhibits showcasing our town's evolution from its founding to the present day. Highlights include:

- **Pioneer Gallery**: Original tools, clothing, and household items from early settlers
- **Industrial Revolution Wing**: Machinery and photographs documenting our town's industrial heritage
- **Modern Era Collection**: Artifacts and stories from the 20th century to today

## Special Programs

We offer guided tours every Saturday at 2:00 PM, and special educational programs for school groups. Check our website for upcoming special exhibitions and events.

## Accessibility

The museum is fully wheelchair accessible with elevators to all floors. Audio guides are available in multiple languages.
