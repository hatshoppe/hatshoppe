## What You'll Find

The Artisan Marketplace brings together over 40 local vendors under one roof, offering:

- **Handmade Crafts**: Pottery, jewelry, textiles, woodwork, and more
- **Fine Art**: Paintings, sculptures, and photography by local artists
- **Gourmet Foods**: Artisan cheeses, baked goods, preserves, and specialty items
- **Vintage Finds**: Carefully curated antiques and collectibles

## Special Events

Join us for special events throughout the year:

- **First Friday Art Walks**: Extended hours with live music and artist demonstrations
- **Seasonal Markets**: Holiday-themed markets featuring special vendors
- **Workshops**: Hands-on classes in various crafts (check schedule online)

## Support Local

Every purchase at the Artisan Marketplace directly supports local artists and small businesses. Many vendors are available to discuss their work and accept custom orders.

## Amenities

The marketplace features a cozy café serving locally roasted coffee and light refreshments. Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the building. Gift wrapping services are complimentary.
